Worth the Hours

Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

Worth the Hours turns prices into working time without linking a bank account or sending your financial inputs to Noia Software.

Information Worth the Hours handles

The app stores the income and work-schedule values you enter, prices you evaluate, parked purchase decisions, bought or skipped outcomes, reclaimed-time statistics, streaks, and app preferences.

Storage and notifications

Your information is stored on your device. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets receive a limited summary through the app's private App Group container. Optional cooling-off reminders are scheduled locally through Apple's notification system and can be disabled in device settings.

Purchases and sharing

Worth the Hours Pro is a one-time In-App Purchase processed by Apple. The app checks the resulting StoreKit entitlement to unlock Pro features; Noia Software does not receive your payment-card details. Share cards are created on your device, and information leaves the app only when you choose a sharing destination.

Data collection and third parties

Noia Software does not receive your income, schedule, prices, purchase history, or decisions. The app does not connect to your bank, use advertising identifiers, run behavioral analytics, or sell or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention and deletion

Settings and decision history remain on your device until you delete decision history, reset the app, or remove the app. App Store purchase records remain with Apple and can be restored.

Financial disclaimer

Worth the Hours is a personal perspective tool, not a financial institution or financial adviser. Calculations depend on the information you enter.

Children

Worth the Hours is not directed to children and Noia Software does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Questions may be sent to support@noiasoftware.com.
