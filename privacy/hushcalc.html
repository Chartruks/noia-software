HushCalc

Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

HushCalc is designed as a private, encrypted vault. Noia Software does not operate an account system, advertising network, analytics service, or cloud storage service for HushCalc.

Information HushCalc handles

HushCalc handles only the content and settings you choose to place in the app, such as photos, videos, Live Photos, files, albums, favorites, your vault passcode configuration, and app preferences.

Where information is stored

Your vault content is encrypted and stored locally on your device. Noia Software cannot view, recover, or decrypt it. Device authentication, when enabled, is handled by Apple and HushCalc does not receive your biometric data.

Transfers, exports, and backups

HushCalc shares or exports content only when you start the action, such as saving an item, transferring it to a nearby Apple device, or creating an encrypted backup archive. A backup remains wherever you choose to save it and is protected by the password you set.

Data collection and third parties

Noia Software does not collect personal information, usage analytics, advertising identifiers, vault metadata, or vault content from HushCalc. The app does not sell or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention and deletion

Content remains on your device until you delete it. Items in Recently Deleted remain there until you remove them permanently or the app clears them according to its displayed retention behavior. Deleting HushCalc removes app data from that device, subject to Apple device backups you control.

Children

HushCalc is not directed to children and Noia Software does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Questions may be sent to support@noiasoftware.com.
