Readium

Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

Readium is designed so your books, reading history, and progress remain under your control.

Information Readium handles

The app may handle books and documents you import, library metadata, bookmarks, reading position, reading-session duration, goals, streaks, achievements, reading statistics, and app preferences.

On-device storage and iCloud

Readium stores app data on your device. If iCloud synchronization is enabled in a version of the app and in your Apple account, eligible library records, progress, and settings may be stored in your private iCloud container so they can stay in sync across your devices. Apple processes that data under its own privacy policy. Noia Software does not have a developer dashboard for reading or inspecting your private iCloud content.

Notifications

Optional reading reminders are delivered through Apple's notification system according to your settings and can be disabled at any time.

Data collection and third parties

Noia Software does not use Readium to collect the contents of your books, your reading history, advertising identifiers, or behavioral analytics, and does not sell or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention and deletion

Books, progress, and settings remain until you remove them or delete the app. If iCloud sync is enabled, you may also need to remove synchronized app data through your Apple account or device settings. Apple may retain backups according to your iCloud settings.

Children

Readium is not directed to children and Noia Software does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Questions may be sent to support@noiasoftware.com.
