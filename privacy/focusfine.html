FocusFine

Privacy Policy

Effective August 27, 2026

FocusFine helps you block distractions and complete focus commitments while keeping your selections and session history private.

Information FocusFine handles

The app stores the focus presets you create, selected Screen Time application or category tokens, session duration, commitment settings, completion history, focused time, streaks, and app preferences.

Screen Time selections

Apple's Screen Time frameworks provide FocusFine with privacy-preserving tokens used to apply shields. Noia Software does not receive a readable list of the apps or categories you select.

Storage

FocusFine data is stored on your device. The current version does not send notifications or schedule focus reminders.

Payments

FocusFine offers optional consumable In-App Purchases that let you end an active commitment early. Selecting a commitment amount does not make a purchase. Apple presents a purchase confirmation only if you later choose to end early and tap Pay & Unlock. Apple processes the transaction, and Noia Software does not receive your payment-card details. The app stores only the verified transaction identifier needed to reconcile that early exit on your device.

Data collection and third parties

Noia Software does not use FocusFine to collect personal information, selected-app identities, advertising identifiers, or behavioral analytics, and does not sell or share personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

Retention and deletion

Session history and settings remain on your device until you clear them or delete the app. Purchase records retained by Apple are governed by Apple's policies and account controls.

Children

FocusFine is not directed to children and Noia Software does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated. Questions may be sent to support@noiasoftware.com.
